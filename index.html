Blackjack Rules (simple)

  • Each round you start with 2 random cards.
  • Number cards are worth their number. Face cards (J/Q/K) are worth 10. Ace counts as 11.
  • Click "NEW CARD" to draw one more card (you can draw up to one extra card — 3 cards total).
  • If your sum is exactly 21 you get Blackjack and win.
  • If your sum goes over 21 you are out of the game (bust).
